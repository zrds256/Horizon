# Product opportunity signal profile

Use this profile for content that provides concrete evidence of an unmet user need, recurring pain point, costly workaround, underserved workflow, meaningful product adoption, switching behavior, churn reason, willingness to pay, or a market shift that creates a plausible product opportunity.

Typical items include first-hand problem descriptions, repeated complaints with context, requests for missing capabilities, discussions of manual work that consumes substantial time or money, evidence that users combine multiple tools to complete one job, and product launches whose response reveals a clear demand pattern or gap.

Do not use this profile for generic startup advice, motivational posts, idea lists without evidence, self-promotion disguised as research, affiliate content, routine product launches with no demand evidence, or unsupported claims about market size.

# Product opportunity signal profile

Use this profile for content that provides concrete evidence of an unmet user need, recurring pain point, costly workaround, underserved workflow, meaningful product adoption, switching behavior, churn reason, willingness to pay, or a market shift that creates a plausible product opportunity.

Typical items include first-hand problem descriptions, repeated complaints with context, requests for missing capabilities, discussions of manual work that consumes substantial time or money, evidence that users combine multiple tools to complete one job, and product launches or discussions that reveal a specific workflow, target user, competitor movement, demand pattern, or possible gap. A launch without usage or purchase evidence is only an early market signal and must not be described as validated demand.

Do not use this profile for generic startup advice, motivational posts, idea lists without evidence, self-promotion disguised as research, affiliate content, launches that do not identify a concrete user or job, or unsupported claims about market size.
